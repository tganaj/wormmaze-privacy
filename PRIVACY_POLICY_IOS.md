# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how "WormMaze" ("the App", "we", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our application on Apple iOS and iPadOS.

## Summary

WormMaze does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data. The App works entirely offline and has no account, ads, analytics, or online features of any kind.

## Information We Collect

**None.** The App does not collect any personal information, from you or automatically.

## Information Stored On Your Device

The App saves your game progress locally, in its own app sandbox:

- Current level, maze layout, score, and unlocked levels
- Selected language

This data:
- never leaves your device,
- is never transmitted to us or to any third party,
- is removed automatically when you delete the App.

## Third-Party Services

**None.** The App does not integrate Game Center, any advertising network, analytics SDK, crash reporter, or any other third-party service.

## App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

The App does not track you and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, because it never accesses the IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers) or any other cross-app/cross-site identifier.

## Permissions and Device Access

The App does not request any iOS permissions or device capabilities:

- No network access is used for gameplay (the one exception is the optional "more games" links in the menu, below)
- No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access
- No Game Center, push notifications, or in-app purchases

## Links to Other Apps

The App's menu contains optional links to the developer's other apps on the Apple App Store and Google Play. Tapping one opens your browser or the respective store app, which is governed by Apple's or Google's own privacy policy, not by this one.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any personal information from any user, including children under 13, and is suitable for a general audience.

## Your Rights

Since the App collects nothing, there is no data of yours for us to access, correct, or delete on your behalf. Deleting the App removes all of its locally stored data (progress, settings) from your device.

## Changes to This Policy

If a future version of the App changes how data is processed (for example, by adding analytics or ads), this policy will be updated and the new version published at the same address, with an updated "Last updated" date above.
